dupeGuruX help
==============

This help document is also available in these languages:

* `French <../fr/index.html>`__

dupeGuruX is a tool to find duplicate files on your computer. It has three
modes, Standard, Music and Picture, with each mode having its own scan types
and little features.

dupeGuruX is a fork of `dupeGuru`_, created by Virgil Dupras and maintained by Andrew Senetar.
As the development of dupeGuru seemed to have come to a halt, dupeGuruX modernizes it (a Qt 6
user interface with PySide6, current Python versions, parallel scanning) and adds new features,
such as :doc:`files missing in reference <missing>` and new scan types. Your settings and data of
dupeGuru are taken over when dupeGuruX starts for the first time.

Although dupeGuruX can easily be used without documentation, reading this file
will help you to master it. If you are looking for guidance for your first
duplicate scan, you can take a look at the :doc:`Quick Start <quick_start>`
section.

It is a good idea to keep dupeGuruX updated. You can download the latest version on its `homepage`_.
